LM741 Inverting Amplifier

A breadboard inverting op-amp whose gain I can adjust by turning a potentiometer.

LM741Op-ampFritzing
StatusFinished
Supply±5 V

Goal

Build and measure a classic inverting amplifier and see the gain formula work on a real circuit.

  • Confirm that gain = −Rf / Rin
  • Draw the circuit in Fritzing
  • Measure input and output on a scope

Materials

PartQtyNotes

How it works

The input signal goes through a 1 kΩ resistor into the inverting input of the op-amp. The potentiometer sits between the output and that input, so turning it changes the gain. The output is the input flipped upside down and multiplied by the gain.

  1. Wire the power. +5 V and −5 V to the op-amp supply pins.
  2. Add Rin and Rf. 1 kΩ in, 10 kΩ pot in the feedback path.
  3. Ground the non-inverting input.
  4. Apply a signal and measure. Compare input and output.
