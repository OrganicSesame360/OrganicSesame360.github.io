How it works

This is an LM386 audio amplifier. The audio comes in through C1 and the volume pot, the LM386 boosts the signal, and C5 passes the amplified signal to the speaker while blocking the DC. The remaining parts like C2, C6, and the R1/C4 components keep the circuit stable and quiet by filtering power noise and preventing oscillation.

  1. Step one. Build the circuit according to the diagram above.
  2. Step two. Move the components from the breadboard to a protoboard to solder and test.
