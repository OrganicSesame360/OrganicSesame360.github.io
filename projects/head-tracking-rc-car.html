Head-Tracking FPV RC Car

An RC car with a camera that follows my head movement, so I can look around while I drive.

Goal

Build an FPV (first-person view) RC car where the camera pans and tilts to match my head.

  • Read head motion with a motion sensor
  • Move two servos to point the camera
  • Stream the camera view to my eyes

How it works

A motion sensor on my head measures how it turns. The microcontroller converts that into servo angles, and the servos move the camera on the car to match.

  1. Wire the sensor. Connect the MPU6050 to the ESP32.
  2. Read the angles. Print them to check they respond to movement.
  3. Drive the servos. Map head angles to servo positions.
  4. Mount the camera. Attach it to the pan/tilt servos on the car.
